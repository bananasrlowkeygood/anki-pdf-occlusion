PDF Occlusion creates image occlusion cards directly from PDF lecture slides. No more importing one image at a time — open a PDF (or several), draw boxes over what you want to memorize, and generate cards in bulk. Built for slide-heavy lectures. From PDF to cards in seconds: auto-detect the text on a slide, draw and marquee-select boxes, group them into a single card, and create everything with one click. Grouped boxes are masked and revealed together with a single clean outline. Features
  • Auto-detect text — auto-place boxes over every text line (T)
  • Groups — multi-select boxes and press G; each group becomes one card. Shift-draw adds new boxes straight into the group
  • Sessions — work saves automatically per PDF. Reopen it later and Create All Cards updates your existing cards in place — review history intact — instead of duplicating them
  • Notes — double-click a box to attach a note, shown under the answer
  • Two occlusion modes — Hide All Show One, or Hide One Show One — settable per slide and per box/group (right-click)
  • Fast editing — marquee select, copy/paste across slides, undo/redo, nudge with arrow keys, pinch-to-zoom
  • Bulk workflow — open multiple PDFs at once, skip slides with Space, create everything with one click
  • Deleted cards clean up their media automatically
Quick start
  1. Tools → PDF Occlusion (or Ctrl+Shift+P from the card editor)
  2. Open a PDF — the arrow next to the button resumes recent sessions
  3. Draw boxes, or click Detect Text
  4. Pick a deck and click Create All Cards
Works on Anki 23.10+ on macOS, Windows, and Linux (Apple Silicon, Intel, and ARM included — the PDF renderer is bundled, nothing to install). Bugs and feature requests: GitHub issues. Full documentation in the README.
